Report Figures

Figures and tables for the report, built with the retractionwatch package.

How countries are counted:

  • Each country: a paper counts once in every country of its authors.
  • Groups: a paper counts once, in a single group:
    • United Kingdom: at least one UK author.
    • Other OECD: no UK author, but at least one author from another OECD country.
    • Rest of World: no author from the UK or another OECD country.
  • Papers with no known country (“Unknown” in Retraction Watch) are left out.

Retraction Watch entries include all notice types (retractions, expressions of concern, corrections and reinstatements). Mass retractions (IEEE 2009–2011, Hindawi 2022–2024) are included.

AI retractions in the 10 OECD countries with most retractions

The 10 OECD countries with the most retractions, excluding mass retractions, as on the library analysis page. Counts and rates cover papers published in 2017–2025, when almost all AI retractions happen, for both the retractions and the SCImago publication counts. A retraction mentions AI when one of its reasons is “Computer-Aided Content” or “Computer-Generated Content”. Intervals are exact 95% confidence intervals (binomial for percentages, Poisson for rates).

Top 10 retraction reasons in the UK and the rest of the OECD

The 10 reasons cited by the most Other OECD retractions from 2017 onwards, as a single list. Each panel shows the yearly proportion of that group’s retractions citing each reason, by retraction year. A retraction can cite several reasons, so the proportions in a year can add up to more than 100%. Years with fewer than 20 retractions (UK) or 80 (Other OECD) are left out, as on the library page.
